Stage checks

What we check before you pay.

Your engineer signs a checklist and sends photos before any payment.

Foundation & plinth

  • Footing size matched to the soil test report

  • Cover blocks and PCC level checked

Stage 2 of 6

RCC columns & slabs

  • Columns plumb within 5 mm

  • Slump test on site for each pour

Stage 3 of 6

Brickwork & plaster

  • Blocks or bricks from the agreed brand and size
  • Wall line and plumb checked every course
  • Chicken mesh at every RCC and wall joint
Payment released after sign-off15%
Stage 4 of 6

Doors, windows & services

  • Frames and shutters checked against your package
  • Window brand, glass and grill as agreed
  • Electrical conduits photographed before plastering

Elevation, terrace & roof

  • Tile and paint brands checked against the bills

Handover

  • Every fitting checked against the package list
  • Drawings, bills and warranty card handed over

Approvals

Plan approval, handled for you.

Approving authority: DTCP, town panchayat or village panchayat, depending on where your plot is. Typical time: 30–60 days. Government fees are paid at actuals and every receipt is shared with you.

Village panchayat approval

Most plots outside town limits. We prepare the plan, file it and follow up until the approval copy is in your hand.

Our fee₹15,000

DTCP / town panchayat approval

For plots in Dharmapuri town, Harur, Palacode and other town panchayats, filed through the single-window portal.

Building with a home loan

Bank-ready papers, from your first quote.

Banks release construction loans in stages. Our stage reports match what they ask for.

How it works

1. Free bank-ready estimate (BOQ) with your quote.

2. The bank releases money stage by stage.

3. We give you a signed report and photos after each stage.

4. You never pay ahead of the loan.

  • Sale deed or patta and chitta for the plot
  • Encumbrance certificate (EC)
  • Approved building plan and permit
  • Our itemised estimate (BOQ) and agreement
  • ID, address proof and income proof
  • Bank statements for the last 6 months

The loan decision is the bank's; we help with the papers.
