Questions from Karimangalam

Straight answers.

What is the house construction cost per sq.ft in Karimangalam?

Our turnkey rates in Karimangalam start at ₹1,950 (Essential), ₹2,300 (Standard) and ₹2,700 (Premium) per sq.ft, excluding GST. Labour with site supervision is ₹399 per sq.ft. Houses under 1,000 sq.ft add ₹125/sq.ft and ground-floor-only houses add ₹100/sq.ft.

Who approves building plans in Karimangalam?

Town panchayat or village panchayat. We prepare the drawings, file the application and share every government fee receipt with you.

What should I know about the ground in Karimangalam?

Red soil with gravel. We do a soil test before the foundation is designed.

How long does it take to build a house in Karimangalam?

A 1,800 sq.ft G+1 house usually takes 6 to 8 months after approval. The agreement has a stage schedule, and if we finish a stage late we credit 0.1% of the contract value per week, up to 2%.
