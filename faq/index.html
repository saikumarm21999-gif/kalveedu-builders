Questions about building with us

What is the house construction cost per sq.ft in 2026?

In Dharmapuri and Krishnagiri our turnkey rates start at ₹1,950 (Essential), ₹2,300 (Standard) and ₹2,700 (Premium) per sq.ft, excluding GST. In Bengaluru they start at ₹2,050, ₹2,400 and ₹2,800. Labour with site supervision is ₹399 per sq.ft in Dharmapuri and ₹499 in Bengaluru. Houses under 1,000 sq.ft add ₹125 per sq.ft and ground-floor-only houses add ₹100 per sq.ft, because kitchens, bathrooms, foundation and roof cost the same whatever the size.

Can you build only the structure and let me finish later?

Yes. Our Structure only option covers the foundation, RCC frame, walls, plastering inside and out, conduits, pipe sleeves and terrace weathering course. It starts at ₹1,399 per sq.ft in Dharmapuri and ₹1,549 in Bengaluru, excluding GST. You finish floors, doors, painting and fittings later.

Can I build with a home loan?

Yes. We give you a bank-ready itemised estimate with every quote, and a signed stage report with photos after each stage, which is what banks ask for before releasing the next part of a construction loan.

Will the rate change after I sign?

No. The rate per sq.ft and every allowance in your package are fixed in the agreement. The total changes only if you change the area or ask for upgrades, and each change is priced in writing before work starts.

What is the difference between turnkey and labour + supervision?

In turnkey we buy all materials and deliver the finished house at a fixed rate. In labour + supervision you buy the materials, we provide the mesthiri team and a site engineer, and we give you a material list and quantities for each stage so nothing is wasted.

Do you build in villages around Dharmapuri?

Yes. We work across Dharmapuri and Krishnagiri districts, including Pennagaram, Palacode, Harur, Pappireddipatti, Karimangalam, Nallampalli, Morappur and nearby villages, and we handle village panchayat approvals.

I live in Bengaluru or abroad. How do I follow the work?

You get a weekly photo and video report on WhatsApp, a video call on request, and stage sign-off can be done by an engineer you nominate. Payments go by bank transfer against each signed stage report.

Can I visit houses you have built?

Yes. Ask for the list of completed homes near your plot. With the owners' permission we arrange a visit so you can see the work and talk to them.

Is there a warranty?

10 years on the structure (foundation, columns, beams, slabs) and 2 years on waterproofing and plumbing leaks, written into the agreement.
